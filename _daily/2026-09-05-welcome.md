GPT-6 Astra released yesterday, and folks are already losing their minds about it.
While I am still of the opinion that those who make good judgement calls
and the ability to understand the systems they work on will never truly be
out of a job, it is not my opinion that matters, it is managements. In addition,
I enjoy the actual technical implementation of specs and problem solutions, 
something which AI is particularly good at. So, I intend on learning and developing
a skillset to help with job security and longevity as time continues.

I've jumped between plenty of different decisions and thoughts about what I should
do, skillsets to develop, whether I should pivot careers entirely, etc. For now,
I have decided to invest time into learning embedded development, and possible robotics.
These fields have core hardware dependencies that make it more difficult for folks to
just vibe it all out. And that hardware is important context for the software that 
needs to be developed, making it more difficult for AI to write correct and efficient
software for production for those systems. At least, those are my thoughts. Of course
providing the AI enough context and direction I'm sure it can product good software 
that does what is needed of it, but again, one needs the skillset and knowledge to
be able to provide those things well.

Anyway, I currently work in Cloud and Mobile Application cybersecurity, so this will
be a very interesting pivot. I hope to remain in cyber, as these systems need 
cybersecurity just as much as any other system, especially as these systems enter
people's homes and lives. Lets see how this goes
